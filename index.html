01 · the game

What SPIDER-TAG is

One of you holds the bag. The other one runs.

Pass the bag by touching them, by a Yoink (click when your ring on them turns red), or by the yank: a homing web-zip onto them from up to 9 m away. The one who gets it is tangled in web for a second and a half.

Your score is how long you held the bag. Lowest at the horn wins the round. Sit on it too long and it heats up: the holder's reach and speed grow until it gets passed, so nobody can start with it and just run.

Everything RadRun has works in a match: swing, zip, wall run, ledge grab, vault, slide, charge leap, dive. Wager matches are 1v1 and best 2 of 3.

02 · playing for

How a match works

  1. Deposit

    Connect a wallet (MetaMask or Rabby; the page adds the network). Approve and deposit. The balance sits in the vault contract under your address, not with us.

  2. Sign once

    One signature lets this browser enter matches up to the stake you pick, for 12 hours, so there's no wallet popup per match. That key can enter matches. It can never withdraw.

  3. Find or invite a match

    Post an offer to the open lobby, send an invite link, or name one address. Both players put up the same stake.

  4. Play best of 3

    Leave mid-series and it counts as a forfeit after 20 seconds. If someone doesn't turn up for round 1, the match is void and nobody pays.

  5. The referee replays it

    Both players' inputs go through the game's own simulation, and that replay decides every round. Not either player's screen.

  6. Payout, 97/3

    The winner's vault balance gets the pot minus 3%, or 1.5% if the winner holds a Radbro. Stakes of 100 each: the winner gets 194, the house 6.

  7. Withdraw any time

    Free balance goes straight from the contract back to your wallet. No server, owner or referee is involved, and pausing the vault doesn't block it.

03 · why it's fair

Check it yourself

Deterministic replays

The same inputs give the same match on any machine. The referee runs the same game code your browser does, on the inputs the relay recorded, so a hacked client can't report its way to a win.

A verify page for every match

Every settled series has a public page that replays the whole thing in your browser and checks the result and its log hash against the chain. Anyone can open it, not just the two players.

Cheating

A series that looks scripted waits for a manual review instead of paying out. New accounts get lower stake caps, and every player card shows its record and rating.

What the vault can't do

Nobody can touch a free balance: not the owner, not the referee, not the server. The referee can only settle a locked match, between its two players, at the fee that match locked in. The owner can set fees up to a hard cap of 5%, set stake caps and pause new matches. It can't move balances or block withdrawals, and the contract can't be upgraded.

If something breaks

A match nobody settles within 24 hours can be refunded by anyone. If the referee is gone, the two players can both sign the same result and settle it between them.

06 · questions

FAQ

Do I need the token to play RadRun?

No. RadRun and SPIDER-TAG, against bots or a friend online, are free. The token is only for wager matches.

What's live right now?

The wager beta on Testnet. It hands you test tokens and a little test ETH, and they have no value. The real coin and the mainnet vault come after.

Which wallet?

MetaMask or Rabby in Chrome, Brave or Firefox. On a phone, open the link in MetaMask's in-app browser. The page adds and switches the network for you.

Do I need ETH?

A little ETH on for approve, deposit and withdraw, well under a cent each. Entering, locking and settling matches is gasless.

What if the server goes down mid-match?

The stakes are locked in the contract, not on a server. You and the other player can both sign the result and settle it, or after 24 hours anyone can refund both stakes.

How much can I stake?

The beta has caps per match and per player, and lower ones for new accounts until they've played a few series.

Where does the 3% go?

To a house address fixed in the vault contract. The contract itself caps the fee at 5%.

Is it audited?

No. The vault has unit, fuzz and invariant tests and its own review, not an outside audit. pons v2's audits are still in progress. Treat all of it as beta.
